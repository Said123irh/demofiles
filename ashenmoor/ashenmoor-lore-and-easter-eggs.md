## 1. THE CRYPT LORE (locked)
- The crypt under Emberfall's chapel is sealed to PROTECT something, not to trap a monster.
- Queen Isolde herself ordered it sealed the night the Sunwell fell. The Keepers were created to guard it. Elder Bram is the last Keeper.
- What it protects: the truth about the Sunwell and the Queen, the proof that Wick's lantern holds her last light, and the ghost of Sir Oswin Hale, who still guards the Queen's door.
- It is hidden from the bad guys: the Hollow King Aldric (wants to put out the last spark) and the Order of the Snuffed Flame (the cult sabotaging Emberfall's lamps).
- Bram's "it is cursed" line is a cover story. A scary rumor kept curious people away for 60 years.
- The golden light behind the Queen's door (first seen in Episode 2) is not a monster. Something inside is reacting to the last spark (Wick's lantern) coming close, and it pulses in the same rhythm. Mysterious first, then a good surprise. (Note: Episode 1 has NO breathing or waking sound in the finished video, so do not refer to it.)
- The key was never meant for Wick. It was stolen long ago from the Keepers and a Gloomhound carried it back toward the lantern. (The hound that did not run in Episode 1 is the one who dropped it. Can return later.)
