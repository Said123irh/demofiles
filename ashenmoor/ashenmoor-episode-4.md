# EPISODE 4: TWO VERSIONS
Series: The Lamplighter of Ashenmoor (English narration)

Episode 3 asked: **A: Hang the lantern on the hook.** or **B: Keep the lantern and step into the dark.**
Animate only the version that wins the vote.

| Version | Title | Plays if Episode 3 vote was |
|---|---|---|
| **4A** | "Return the Light" | A: Hang the lantern on the hook |
| **4B** | "The Lady in the Lantern" | B: Keep the lantern |

**Story spine beat (both versions):** Wick learns his lantern is special. Its flame is a piece of the Sunwell, and someone is inside it. She is not named yet and cannot speak yet.

Both versions end with the same scene and the same choice.

**Choice at the end (both versions):** A: Chase the hooded figure. B: Relight the lamps and protect the village.

**Length:** about 4 minutes | **Type:** Standard
**New thing this episode:** the shrine (the room behind the door, lit up). In 4B it is only seen through Ember Sight, which is the same background with a color tint.

**Path lines:** a few lines change with the **Episode 2** vote (A: the door is shut and the Bone-Wight is rebuilding itself; B: Sir Rattles is holding the door open). They are marked "if Episode 2 was A / B". All of them are voice only or a single still.

## VERSION 4A: "RETURN THE LIGHT"

### TITLE CARD + RECAP (0:00 to 0:25)
*Title card template: "EPISODE 4: RETURN THE LIGHT". Recap over stills from Episode 3: the Bone-Wight, the stone door, the hook and the words "Return the light."*

**NARRATOR:** Last time: past the Bone-Wight, in a room of total dark, Wick found a hook and four words. Return the light. You voted A. Wick is about to face his biggest fear.

### SCENE 1: Letting go (0:25 to 1:05)
*Mostly still. Close up on Wick's hands holding the lantern up to the hook. They shake.*

**WICK:** It's fine. It's just the dark. The dark is just the light... taking a nap.

*He hangs the lantern on the hook. He lets go. He steps back.*

*The flame shrinks to a tiny dot. The screen goes almost fully black. We only see the dot and Wick's two eyes.*

**WICK:** (very small voice) I take it back. I take it all back. Hello? Lantern? Buddy?

**NARRATOR:** For one long breath, Wick Marrow stands in the thing he fears most. And he does not run.

### SCENE 2: The shrine wakes up (1:05 to 1:50)
*The hook glows. Lines of gold light run out from the stand across the floor and up the walls, like roots. Old lamps on the walls light one by one (simple flicker on, left to right).*

*The room is revealed: a round shrine. On the walls, a painting of a queen with her lantern, and seven figures standing around her, faded to silhouettes.*

**WICK:** Oh. Oh wow. Okay. The dark was hiding a really nice room.

**NARRATOR:** Seven figures stand around the queen. Nobody has said their names in a hundred years. Hold on to that.

### SCENE 3: The face in the flame (1:50 to 2:40)
*Slow zoom on the lantern on the hook. The flame grows tall. Inside it, a woman's face appears: a crown, eyes closed. Then her eyes open. She looks right at Wick.*

*Her lips move. No sound comes out.*

**WICK:** ...Hello?

*Under the hook, more words light up in the stone. Insert still.*

**WICK:** (reading) "The last spark sleeps in a Lamplighter's hand."

**NARRATOR:** The flame in Wick's lantern is not just a flame. It is a piece of the Sunwell. The last piece. And someone is inside it.

**WICK:** I've had you since I was six. I've sung in front of you. I've sung BADLY in front of you.

### SCENE 4: The price (2:40 to 3:00)
*Far above, muffled: a bell ringing. Clang. Clang. Clang. The shrine lamps flicker.*

**NARRATOR:** But light is never free. While the lantern burned on the hook, it pulled at every flame nearby. Including the lamps of Emberfall.

**WICK:** The village bell. The lamps! Dark take it!

*He grabs the lantern off the hook. The woman's face fades. The shrine goes dim.*

*Path line (voice over a still of the stone door):*
- **If Episode 2 was A:** The stone door that would not open swings wide by itself as the lantern comes near. In the hall, a half built Bone-Wight is still clicking itself together. Wick runs past it. Fast.
- **If Episode 2 was B:** Sir Rattles is still at the door. "Go, little light." Wick runs past him. "Thanks, Sir Rattles!"

*Then: SHARED ENDING.*

## VERSION 4B: "THE LADY IN THE LANTERN"

### TITLE CARD + RECAP (0:00 to 0:25)
*Title card template: "EPISODE 4: THE LADY IN THE LANTERN". Recap over stills from Episode 3: the Bone-Wight, the stone door, the hook and the words "Return the light."*

**NARRATOR:** Last time: past the Bone-Wight, in a room of total dark, Wick found a hook and four words. Return the light. You voted B. Wick is not letting go.

### SCENE 1: No deal (0:25 to 1:05)
*Mostly still. Wick pulls the lantern close to his chest.*

**WICK:** Return the light. Nope. Sorry, stone. Me and this lantern have a deal. I don't let go of it, and it doesn't let me get eaten in the dark.

*He holds the lantern high and steps past the stand into the black. The light makes only a small circle around him (dark ring overlay from Episode 3).*

**NARRATOR:** Holding on feels safe. But a small light in a big dark only shows you a very small part of the room.

### SCENE 2: Ember Sight (1:05 to 1:50)
*Close up on Wick's eyes. They glow orange. Color flash: the shrine background in dark purple with orange outlines.*

*Path line (voice only):*
- **If Episode 2 was A:** **NARRATOR:** His head is still spinning from the Flare. He uses Ember Sight anyway. That is either very brave or very silly.
- **If Episode 2 was B:** **NARRATOR:** Ember Sight makes his head spin. Wick uses it anyway.

*Through Ember Sight we see the round shrine: a painting of a queen with her lantern, and seven faded figures standing around her. Words glow on the wall.*

**WICK:** (reading) "The last spark sleeps in a Lamplighter's hand."

*He looks down at his own lantern.*

### SCENE 3: The lady in the lantern (1:50 to 2:40)
*Slow zoom into the lantern, still in Ember Sight colors. Inside the flame, very small, a woman with a crown is curled up, asleep. Her eyes open. She looks right at Wick.*

**NARRATOR:** The flame in Wick's lantern is not just a flame. It is a piece of the Sunwell. The last piece. And someone is inside it.

**WICK:** There's a lady in my lantern. There is a LADY. In my LANTERN.

*She tries to speak. No sound comes out.*

**WICK:** How long have you been in there? I've had you since I was six. I've sung in front of you. I've sung BADLY in front of you.

*The Ember Sight colors start to swirl. Wick sways.*

### SCENE 4: The price (2:40 to 3:00)
*Fade to black. Silence. Then, muffled from far above: a bell ringing. Clang. Clang. Clang.*

*Fade in. Wick is lying on the floor. He sits up fast.*

**NARRATOR:** Ember Sight takes a lot out of a person. Wick was out cold. Not for long. But long enough.

**WICK:** The village bell. How long was I... Dark take it!

*Path line (voice over a still of the stone door):*
- **If Episode 2 was A:** The stone door that would not open swings wide by itself as the lantern comes near. In the hall, a half built Bone-Wight is still clicking itself together. Wick runs past it. Fast.
- **If Episode 2 was B:** Sir Rattles is still at the door. "Go, little light." Wick runs past him. "Thanks, Sir Rattles!"

*Then: SHARED ENDING.*

## SHARED ENDING (both versions)

### SCENE 5: Out of the chapel (3:00 to 3:25)
*Reused background: Emberfall at night. Wick climbs out of the chapel floor. Only three lamps are still lit. Fog everywhere.*

*Across the square, a hooded figure stands on a ladder by a lamp. It holds a long brass pole with a little cup on the end. It presses the cup over the flame. The lamp goes out.*

**WICK:** (whispering) Somebody's putting them out. They never went out on their own. Somebody's been doing it.

*At the forest edge, red pixel eyes appear in the fog. Gloomhounds, heading for the dark side of the village.*

### CHOICE SCREEN (3:25 to 4:00)
*Freeze frame. Choice template with big pixel buttons.*

**NARRATOR:** The person putting out the lamps is right there. And so are the Gloomhounds. Wick cannot stop both. What does he do?

> **OPTION A: Chase the hooded figure.** (Find out who is doing this. But the village is left in the dark.)
>
> **OPTION B: Relight the lamps and protect the village.** (Keep everyone safe. But the hooded figure may get away.)

**NARRATOR:** You decide. Vote in the Community tab, or comment A or B. May your lamp never flicker.

*Fade to black. One pixel flame flickers.*

## PRODUCTION NOTES
- **New assets:** shrine background (round room, queen painting with seven silhouettes), the face in the flame (one still plus eyes open frame), the hooded figure silhouette with a brass snuffer pole, the words insert still.
- **Reused:** stand and hook, dark ring overlay, Ember Sight color flash (from Episode 2), stone door still, Emberfall night background, Gloomhound eyes, Wick sprite sheet.
- **Action scenes:** none in either version. This is the quiet reveal episode, carried by slow zooms and lights flickering on.
- **The woman in the flame is Queen Isolde,** but she is NOT named on screen yet and she cannot speak. Keep her a mystery. She is a glimpse, not a new character.
- **The seven figures are the Lantern Seven.** Not named yet. A seed for later arcs.
- **The hooded figure is a member of the Order of the Snuffed Flame.** Keep them a silhouette. Not named yet.
- **Story consequence to track:**
  - 4A: Wick faced total darkness and did not run (growth for his flaw). The lantern pulled light from the village, which is partly why lamps are out.
  - 4B: Wick held on. He fainted from Ember Sight and lost time, which is partly why lamps are out.
