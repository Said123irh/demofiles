# EPISODE 2: TWO VERSIONS
Series: The Lamplighter of Ashenmoor (English narration)

Episode 1 asked: **A: Go down into the crypt.** or **B: Show Elder Bram the key.**
Animate only the version that wins the vote.

| Version | Title | Plays if Episode 1 vote was |
|---|---|---|
| **2A** | "Down the Stair" | A: Go down into the crypt |
| **2B** | "What Bram Hid" | B: Show Elder Bram the key |

Both versions end with the same scene and the same choice, so Episode 3 only needs two versions.

**Choice at the end (both versions):** A: Fight the Bone-Wight with a Flare. B: Hold up the lantern and talk to it.

**Length:** about 4 minutes | **Type:** Standard
**New thing this episode:** the crypt (new location). The Bone-Wight appears only as a silhouette with two glowing eyes; its full sprite is built in Episode 3.

## VERSION 2A: "DOWN THE STAIR"

### TITLE CARD + RECAP (0:00 to 0:25)
*Title card template: "EPISODE 2: DOWN THE STAIR". Then recap over stills from Episode 1: dark lamps, Gloomhound eyes, the glowing key, the hidden stair.*

**NARRATOR:** Last time: the lamps of Emberfall were failing. Gloomhounds crept out of the fog. And Wick found a glowing key that opened a stair under the chapel. You voted A. So... down we go.

### SCENE 1: The top of the stair (0:25 to 1:05)
*Mostly still. Wick at the top of a broken stone stair under the chapel floor. Only his lantern lights the first few steps. Below is black.*

**WICK:** Okay. Stairs. I like stairs. Stairs are just a floor that went on an adventure.

*A step creaks under his boot. He freezes.*

**WICK:** Nope. Hate stairs now.

**NARRATOR:** Wick has one rule about the dark. Do not go into it. He is about to break that rule.

*He looks back up at the chapel door. Through it, one warm lamp glows in the village.*

**WICK:** (to himself) Keep your wick trimmed, Marrow. In and out. Nobody needs to know.

*He steps down. The light at the top of the frame shrinks to a dot. Fade.*

### SCENE 2: The hall of pictures (1:05 to 1:50)
*New location: the crypt hall. Slow pan along a stone wall covered in faded paintings. Parallax: pillars in front, wall behind.*

**NARRATOR:** Under Emberfall lies something nobody alive remembers. A long hall of pictures, painted before the sun was stolen.

*Pan to a painting of a tall white tower with a little sun at the top.*

**WICK:** That's the Sunwell. Elder Bram tells that story every winter. Badly. With a lot of coughing.

*Pan to the next painting: a queen in a crown, holding up a small lantern on a stick. Wick lifts his own lantern beside it. Slow zoom on the two lanterns side by side. They are the same.*

**WICK:** Same stick. Same little cage. Same... everything.

*His lantern flickers once, like a heartbeat.*

**WICK:** Huh. Lots of lanterns look like this. Probably. Right?

*It flickers twice.*

**WICK:** That was not a yes.

### SCENE 3: Ember Sight (1:50 to 2:40)
*The end of the hall is pure black. Wick stops. He can hear a slow scrape. Scrape. Scrape.*

**NARRATOR:** Wick has a trick he does not use much. Ember Sight. It lets him see hidden things in the dark. It also makes his head spin like he just turned around fifty times.

*Close up on Wick's eyes. They glow orange. Color flash: the screen turns dark purple with orange outlines.*

*Through Ember Sight we see the end of the hall: a big stone door with the same flame symbol as the key. In front of it stands a tall shape with a spear. Perfectly still.*

**WICK:** (whispering) There's a guy. A bone guy. A bone guy with a spear. Why is it always a spear?

*The color flash ends. Wick wobbles and grabs a pillar.*

**WICK:** Whoa. Okay. Floor is moving. Floor, please stop moving.

## VERSION 2B: "WHAT BRAM HID"

### TITLE CARD + RECAP (0:00 to 0:25)
*Title card template: "EPISODE 2: WHAT BRAM HID". Then recap over stills from Episode 1: dark lamps, Gloomhound eyes, the glowing key, Bram in his doorway.*

**NARRATOR:** Last time: the lamps of Emberfall were failing. Gloomhounds crept out of the fog. And Wick found a glowing key to a stair under the chapel. You voted B. Time to wake up Elder Bram.

### SCENE 1: A knock at night (0:25 to 1:05)
*Reused background: Bram's doorway from Episode 1, night tint. Wick knocks. The door opens. Bram is in a nightcap.*

**WICK:** Elder! Sorry. I know it's late. Don't be mad. Okay, be a little mad. But look.

*He holds up the key. It glows. Bram's face drains of color (simple color shift on his sprite).*

**BRAM:** Where did you find that?

**WICK:** A Gloomhound dropped it. Which, by the way, I did not know they could carry things. Do they have pockets?

*Bram does not laugh. He steps outside and pulls the door shut behind him.*

**BRAM:** Not inside. Walls have ears in this village. Walk with me.

### SCENE 2: The last Keeper (1:05 to 1:50)
*Reused background: the village well at night. Mostly still. Slow zoom on Bram.*

*Bram pulls a thin chain out of his shirt. On it hangs a small iron pendant with the same flame symbol as the key.*

**BRAM:** Sixty years ago, I made a promise. I am a Keeper of the Crypt. The last one. There used to be twelve of us.

**WICK:** Twelve? In Emberfall? We can't even find twelve people to sing at the harvest.

**BRAM:** We guard what sleeps under the chapel. A door. And the thing behind that door.

**WICK:** What thing?

**BRAM:** I do not know. Keepers are not allowed to open it. We are only allowed to wait.

**NARRATOR:** Bram has been waiting for sixty years. That is a long time to wait for anything. Even soup.

**BRAM:** (looking at the dark lamps) The lamps are dying, Wick. Without the Sunwell, Emberfall will not live through Frostdark. If that key has come back now... the waiting is over.

### SCENE 3: The map and the warning (1:50 to 2:40)
*Reused background: the chapel and the hidden stair from Episode 1. Bram hands Wick a folded paper. Insert shot (one still): an old map of the crypt in red ink, the inner door marked with the flame symbol.*

**BRAM:** My knees will not take those stairs. But this map will. Follow the left wall. Do not touch the pictures. And at the end, there is a guard.

**WICK:** A guard? Like Old Marl?

**BRAM:** Not like Old Marl. This one stays awake.

*Bram grips Wick's shoulder.*

**BRAM:** Whatever you find down there, Wick, do not let go of that lantern.

**WICK:** Why would I let go of my lantern?

*Bram does not answer. Wick goes down the stair.*

*Quick pan through the crypt hall (same pan as Version 2A, Scene 2, sped up). Wick has his nose in the map.*

**WICK:** Left wall. Left wall. Don't touch the pictures. Left wall.

*For half a second the pan passes the painting of the queen with the lantern. Wick does not look up. (Easter egg for sharp eyed viewers.)*

## SHARED ENDING (both versions)

### SCENE 4: The guard at the door (2:40 to 3:20)
*The end of the crypt hall. The big stone door with the flame symbol. In front of it, in shadow, a tall silhouette with a spear. Two small cold blue lights where its eyes should be.*

*The eyes flare. The shape takes one step forward and lowers the spear. Screen shake, small.*

**BONE-WIGHT:** (a voice like dry leaves) None... pass... the Queen's door.

**WICK (Version 2A line):** It talks. The bone guy talks. Great. Love that.

**WICK (Version 2B line):** Bram said it stays awake. Bram was right. I hate it when Bram is right.

*Wick's lantern glows brighter on its own. The silhouette stops. Its head tilts, slowly, like it is trying to remember something.*

**NARRATOR:** This is a Bone-Wight. A soldier of old Eldmere. It has guarded this door for a hundred years, loyal to a queen who is long gone. And right now, it is staring at Wick's lantern.

### CHOICE SCREEN (3:20 to 4:00)
*Freeze frame. Choice template with big pixel buttons.*

**NARRATOR:** The door is right there. So is the spear. Wick has one big Flare in him, maybe. What does he do?

> **OPTION A: Fight it. Blast it with a Flare.** (Fast. But it will leave Wick worn out, alone in the dark.)
>
> **OPTION B: Hold up the lantern and talk to it.** (No fighting. But if it will not listen, he is too close to run.)

**NARRATOR:** You decide. Vote in the Community tab, or comment A or B. May your lamp never flicker.

*Fade to black. One pixel flame flickers.*

## PRODUCTION NOTES
- **New assets:** crypt hall background (pillars layer plus wall layer), stone door, Bone-Wight silhouette with eye glow, two mural stills (Sunwell, queen with lantern). Version 2B adds one map insert still.
- **Reused:** Episode 1 stills for recap, Emberfall night background, Bram's doorway, the well, chapel and stair. Wick sprite sheet.
- **Action scenes:** 2A has one light one (Scene 3, Ember Sight color flash). 2B has none. Shared Scene 4 uses only a step and a small screen shake.
- **Lore planted:** the queen's lantern matches Wick's (2A clearly, 2B as an Easter egg). Bram is a Keeper (2B only; in 2A this stays a secret for later). The lantern seems alive.
- **Note on the plan in the master file:** the original Episode 2A plan ended with three options (fight, trick, run) and 2B ended with "leave tonight or wait for morning". Both now end with the same two options so the series follows the "exactly 2 choices" rule and Episode 3 only needs two versions. Bram's winter warning is kept in 2B, Scene 2.
