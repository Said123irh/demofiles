# EPISODE 1: "THE LAST LAMP IN EMBERFALL"
Series: The Lamplighter of Ashenmoor (English narration)

**Choice at the end:** A: Go down into the crypt. B: Show Elder Bram the key.

Note: every later episode opens with a 10 second recap. Episode 1 has none because it is the start.

**Length:** about 4 minutes | **Style:** pixel art, warm lantern glow vs dark blue night
**Narrator:** calm, a bit mysterious, a bit funny

### SCENE 1: Title card (0:00 to 0:15)
*Black screen. A single pixel flame appears. Title: "THE LAMPLIGHTER OF ASHENMOOR".*

**NARRATOR:** A hundred years ago, the sun was stolen from the sky. People say the world has been getting darker ever since. People say a lot of things. But tonight, in one small village, a boy is about to find out they were right.

### SCENE 2: Emberfall at dusk (0:15 to 1:00)
*Wide shot. A tiny village of pixel houses, a wooden chapel, a well, a forest edge. Eight street lamps. Four are dark.*

**NARRATOR:** This is Emberfall. Eight lamps keep it safe. Last year, all eight were lit. Tonight... four.

*Cut to WICK climbing a ladder, lighting a lamp with his lantern. He is humming a bad tune.*

**WICK:** (muttering) One lamp, two lamp, three lamp... why do they keep going out? Is it me? It's probably me.

*Lamp number five flickers and goes out as he lights lamp six.*

**WICK:** Okay, it's definitely not me. That one was already out! ...Right?

*An old man, ELDER BRAM, watches from a doorway.*

**BRAM:** Wick. Come inside. It is nearly night.

**WICK:** Just two more, Elder.

**BRAM:** (quietly) You said that yesterday. And the lamp by the chapel went out while you said it.

### SCENE 3: The strange thing (1:00 to 1:50)
*Wick is alone, walking to the chapel lamp. Fog creeps in. His lantern glows brighter than it should. The nearby lamp flame leans toward it, like it is drawn to it.*

**NARRATOR:** Here is something Wick has never noticed. Wherever he walks, the other lamps lean toward him. As if they want to say something.

*He stops. Looks at the lantern.*

**WICK:** ...Huh. You've been warm all day. Warmer than usual.

*The lantern flickers once. Like a heartbeat.*

**WICK:** Okay. That's weird. I'm choosing to not think about that.

### SCENE 4: The first danger (1:50 to 2:50)
*Dark forest edge. Red pixel eyes appear in the fog. Three GLOOMHOUNDS slink out, shadows shaped like wolves. They are creeping toward the village.*

**NARRATOR:** Gloomhounds. They hate light. They love an unlit lamp.

*Wick freezes. His hands shake. The lantern glows.*

**WICK:** (whispering) Don't look scared. Don't look scared. They can smell it. Do they smell it? I smell like soup.

*The hounds step closer. Wick lifts the lantern. The glow hits them. They whimper and back away, but one hound does not move.*

**NARRATOR:** The hounds are afraid of the light. Mostly. Wick has a choice. And it is not his alone to make.

### SCENE 5: The discovery (2:50 to 3:30)
*The hounds retreat. As they flee, one drops something: a rusted iron key with a flame symbol on it, glowing faintly.*

*Wick picks it up. The key points toward the chapel. Under the chapel floor, a hidden stair reveals itself, half collapsed.*

**WICK:** A key... to the crypt?! Nobody has opened that in sixty years! Elder Bram says it's cursed. He also says carrots are cursed, though.

*From deep underground, a low sound. Like breathing. Like something very old, waking up.*

### SCENE 6: The first choice (3:30 to 4:00)
*Freeze frame. Big pixel buttons.*

**NARRATOR:** The night is getting darker. The lamps are failing. The key is in Wick's hand. What does he do?

> **OPTION A: Go down into the crypt.** (Dangerous. Might find answers. Might find worse.)
>
> **OPTION B: Wake Elder Bram and show him the key.** (Safe. But Bram has been hiding something.)

**NARRATOR:** You decide. Vote in the Community tab, or comment A or B. See you tomorrow.

*Fade to black. One pixel flame flickers.*
