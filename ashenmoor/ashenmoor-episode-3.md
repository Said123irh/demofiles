| **3A** | "Flare" | A: Fight it |

## VERSION 3A: "FLARE"

### TITLE CARD + RECAP (0:00 to 0:25)
*Title card template: "EPISODE 3: FLARE". Recap over stills from Episode 2: the crypt hall, the queen painting, the Bone-Wight's glowing eyes.*

**NARRATOR:** Last time: deep under the chapel, Wick found a door, and a Bone-Wight guarding it. You voted A. Fight. Let's hope Wick has a good arm.

### SCENE 1: The wind up (0:25 to 1:05)
*Mostly still. The Bone-Wight steps out of the shadow for the first time: tall skeleton, rusted royal armor, a long spear. Wick plants his big boots.*

**NARRATOR:** A Flare is Wick's biggest trick. One huge burst of light. It is also the most tiring thing he can do. Afterward, he can barely lift a spoon.

**WICK:** (to the lantern) Okay. You and me. Big light. Biggest light. On three.

*The Bone-Wight raises its spear.*

**WICK:** One...

*The spear starts to come down.*

**WICK:** THREE!

### SCENE 2: The blast (1:05 to 1:50)
*Action scene. Impact frames: Wick swings the lantern up (wind up frame). Full white flash with screen shake. Then the result frame: the Bone-Wight bursts into a pile of bones. The spear clatters across the floor.*

*Silence. Dust drifts down through the lantern light.*

**WICK:** (breathing hard) Did... did I win? I think I won. I have never won anything. I once lost a staring contest to a goat.

*His knees give out. He sits down hard.*

### SCENE 3: The dim light (1:50 to 2:40)
*The lantern is very small now. The dark creeps in from the edges of the screen (a slowly closing dark ring).*

**WICK:** No. No no no. Stay lit. Please stay lit. I can do bones. I can't do dark.

**NARRATOR:** Here is something Wick does not know yet. His lantern has never gone out. Not in rain. Not in snow. Not even the time he dropped it down the well when he was nine.

*The flame shrinks to a dot... and holds. Then it slowly grows back. The dark ring pulls away.*

**WICK:** (shaky laugh) Okay. Good. Good lantern. Best lantern.

*Behind him: click. Wick turns. The skull in the pile of bones has turned to look at him. Click. Click. A finger bone slides toward an arm bone.*

**WICK:** Oh, come on! That's cheating!

### SCENE 4: The door (2:40 to 3:20)
*Wick stumbles to the stone door and pushes the key into the flame symbol. The door grinds open. He staggers through.*

*Behind him, the bones are clicking together faster. Wick turns as the stone door slams shut by itself. Screen shake. Then total quiet.*

**NARRATOR:** The good news: the Bone-Wight cannot follow him. The bad news: the door is shut, and it does not open from this side.

*Then: SHARED ENDING.*

## VERSION 3B: "THE QUEEN'S SOLDIER"

### TITLE CARD + RECAP (0:00 to 0:25)
*Title card template: "EPISODE 3: THE QUEEN'S SOLDIER". Recap over stills from Episode 2: the crypt hall, the queen painting, the Bone-Wight's glowing eyes.*

**NARRATOR:** Last time: deep under the chapel, Wick found a door, and a Bone-Wight guarding it. You voted B. Talk to it. Let's hope it likes conversation.

### SCENE 1: Hello, bones (0:25 to 1:05)
*Mostly still. The Bone-Wight steps out of the shadow for the first time: tall skeleton, rusted royal armor, a long spear. Wick slowly lifts the lantern with both hands.*

**WICK:** (voice cracking) Hi. Hello. I'm Wick. I'm a Lamplighter. I light lamps. It's in the name.

*The spear stays pointed at him.*

**WICK:** I'm also very bad at fighting, if that helps. I once lost a staring contest to a goat.

### SCENE 2: The light remembers (1:05 to 1:50)
*Slow zoom on the Bone-Wight's face. The lantern flickers once, like a heartbeat. The Bone-Wight's eye lights change from cold blue to warm gold.*

*The spear lowers. The Bone-Wight drops to one knee (one new pose). Its armor creaks.*

**BONE-WIGHT:** Ma... jesty.

*Wick looks behind himself. Nobody there.*

**WICK:** Where?

**NARRATOR:** The Bone-Wight is not bowing to Wick. It is bowing to the lantern.

### SCENE 3: Sir Rattles (1:50 to 2:40)
*Mostly still, two shot. The Bone-Wight stays kneeling.*

**BONE-WIGHT:** A hundred years... I kept the door. Now the light returns. You may pass, little light.

**WICK:** Thank you. Um. Sir. Bones. Do you have a name?

*Long pause. The Bone-Wight tilts its head.*

**BONE-WIGHT:** ...I have forgotten it.

**WICK:** That's okay. I'll call you Sir Rattles. Just until you remember.

*The Bone-Wight thinks about this. It nods once. Rattle.*

**NARRATOR:** Sir Rattles. The skeleton who guards the queen's door. Remember him. He will remember you.

### SCENE 4: The door (2:40 to 3:20)
*Sir Rattles stands and pushes the stone door open with one hand. Warm air comes out. Wick steps through. The door stays open behind him, with Sir Rattles standing in it like a guard.*

**SIR RATTLES:** Go in. I will hold the door.

**WICK:** Great. Good. Thank you. Please do not stab anything while I'm gone. Unless it's bad. Then stab it.

*Then: SHARED ENDING.*

## SHARED ENDING (both versions, 3:20 to 4:00 with the choice)

### THE ROOM BEHIND THE DOOR
*Inside is total darkness. Only Wick's lantern lights a small circle. In that circle: a stone stand, waist high, with an iron hook on top. The hook is shaped exactly to hold his lantern. Words are carved under it.*

**WICK:** (reading) "Return... the light."

*Wick looks at the hook. Then at the lantern. Then at the dark around him. His hand shakes.*

**WICK (if Episode 1 was A):** If I hang it up, I'm standing in the dark. Me. In the dark. On purpose.

**WICK (if Episode 1 was B):** Bram said don't let go of it. But the stone says give it back. Somebody here is wrong, and I really hope it's the stone.

### CHOICE SCREEN
*Freeze frame. Choice template with big pixel buttons.*

**NARRATOR:** The stone says to give the light back. Every part of Wick says hold on. What does he do?

> **OPTION A: Hang the lantern on the hook.** (Do what the stone says. But Wick will be standing in the dark.)
>
> **OPTION B: Keep the lantern and step into the dark.** (Safe for now. But the stone did say "return".)

**NARRATOR:** You decide. Vote in the Community tab, or comment A or B. Keep your wick trimmed.

*Fade to black. One pixel flame flickers.*

## PRODUCTION NOTES
- **New assets:** Bone-Wight sprite (idle, spear raise; plus collapse frame for 3A or kneel frame for 3B), stone stand with hook.
- **Reused:** crypt hall background, stone door, dark ring overlay (also used again in Episode 4), Wick sprite sheet, Episode 2 stills for recap.
- **Action scenes:** 3A has one (Scene 2, impact frames). 3B has none, so it is the cheaper version.
- **Story consequence to track:**
  - 3A: the Bone-Wight was smashed but is putting itself back together, and the door is shut behind Wick. It may come back angry later.
  - 3B: the Bone-Wight is now Sir Rattles, a friend who guards the door. A spared creature that can return to help later.
- **Lore planted:** the lantern never goes out (3A says it out loud). The Bone-Wight serves the light in the lantern (3B says it out loud).
