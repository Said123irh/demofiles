### SCENE 2: HELLO, SKELETON (0:13 to 1:24)

*The end of the hall. Wick is small in the upper half of the frame, the Sentry stands in front of the iron door. Wick slowly lowers the lantern to his side. The lantern pulses. The door pulses. Same rhythm.*

**WICK:** *(whispering to himself)* Okay. Talking. Talking is just fighting with fewer sparks.

*He clears his throat and takes one small step forward.*

**WICK:** Hello. I am Wick Marrow. Lamplighter of Emberfall. I light lamps. Mostly small ones.

*Quick cut: the Sentry's helmet tilts.*

**SENTRY:** ...Wick Marrow.

**WICK:** Yes! That's me. Hello.

**SENTRY:** WHO GAVE YOU THE LIGHT?

*Wick opens his mouth. Closes it. Looks at the lantern.*

**WICK:** Nobody gave it to me. I mean, somebody must have. But I don't know who. I have just always had it.

**NARRATOR:** Which was true, and a little odd. Wick had never wondered about it before.

*The Sentry lowers his sword until the tip touches the floor. His cracked armor creaks. When he speaks again, his voice is quiet for the first time.*

**SENTRY:** The Queen's light does not belong to whoever asks for it. It chooses. And it chose you.

**WICK:** Me? *(nervous laugh)* It could have picked someone taller.

**SENTRY:** Listen well, Lamplighter. Others hunt that light. They have hunted it for a long time. Keep the key close.

*Wick pats his pocket, then holds up the rusted key with the flame symbol.*

**WICK:** A Gloomhound gave me this.

**SENTRY:** A hound.

**WICK:** It dropped it and walked away. Very politely, for a hound.

*The Sentry goes still. Long beat.*

**SENTRY:** ...The key must have guided the hound to the lamp.

**NARRATOR:** Gloomhounds hate light. Yet one had carried a key straight toward it. Strange.

### SCENE 3: THE DOOR OPENS (1:24 to 1:51)

*The Sentry steps aside, two stiff steps. His knees creak very loudly (funny sound). No close-up: the moment stays in the wide shot.*

**WICK:** Is that your knee?

**SENTRY:** A hundred years of standing, Lamplighter.

**WICK:** Fair.

*The flame symbol on the iron door lights up gold. The door swings inward on its own, slowly, dust falling. Golden light spills across the floor, pulsing in time with the lantern.*

**WICK:** Door is open. Door is open and also very dark inside.

*He looks at the lantern.*

**WICK:** Good lantern.

*The Sentry sheathes his sword and steps in behind Wick. Tiny clack, clack, clack of bones.*

**NARRATOR:** Wick did not like the dark. But Wick had a lantern. And now, apparently, a skeleton friend.

---

### SCENE 4: THE KNIGHT WHO SLEEPS (1:51 to 2:22)

*A small round stone room. Bare walls. In the middle, a stone bier. On it lies a ghost knight in old Eldmere armor, pale blue and a little see-through, very still, hands folded over a sword. Around the hands is a faint ring of gold light, pulsing in the exact lantern rhythm. In the far background of the shot, tiny and easy to miss: a white moth.*

**WICK:** *(whispering)* He glows at my speed too. Everything down here has my heartbeat.

**NARRATOR:** This was Sir Oswin Hale. The knight who still guards a door.

*Name card: "SIR OSWIN HALE. The knight who still guards a door."*

**SENTRY:** *(almost proud)* The finest sword in all of Eldmere.

**WICK:** Is he...?

**SENTRY:** Asleep. Since the night the Sunwell went dark.

*The white moth drifts across the lantern light, once, then slips into a crack in the wall and is gone.*

**NARRATOR:** A small white moth fluttered by. A sealed room should not have moths. Nobody noticed. We did. Let's keep an eye on that.

*Wick steps up to the bier and holds the lantern out in front of him, careful and slow. The gold ring around the knight's hands pulses faster. And faster.*

### SCENE 5: GOOD MORNING (2:22 to 3:36)

*Oswin's eyes snap open, pale blue. He sits straight up. His armor CLANKS loudly. Wick yelps, jumps back, the lantern swings, and he catches it.*

**WICK:** AH! Hello! Sorry! I'm Wick! Please don't be a monster!

**WICK:** ...Wait. You're a ghost. Ghosts are not allowed to clank.

**OSWIN:** *(grumpy and scratchy)* I clank with style. Is it morning?

*Beat.*

**SENTRY:** SIR OSWIN!

*The Sentry salutes so sharply that his helmet slips down over one eye.*

**OSWIN:** Soldier. Why are you standing so crooked?

**SENTRY:** A hundred years, sir.

**OSWIN:** ...A hundred years of what?

**SENTRY:** Standing, sir.

*Oswin sits very still for a moment.*

**OSWIN:** ...Did I miss anything?

**NARRATOR:** A little.

**WICK:** Define "a little."

*Oswin turns and sees the lantern. The pale fire in his eyes softens.*

**OSWIN:** That light. She said it would come back one day. She said to wait for the warm one.

**WICK:** She?

**OSWIN:** *(gently)* The Queen.

**WICK:** Queen Isolde? The one on the wall out there, with the lantern like mine?

*Oswin swings off the bier and takes a step toward Wick. A chain of pale light flashes tight from a cuff at his ankle to an iron ring on the bier, and drags him back onto the stone. The chain stays clearly visible, running along the bier, for the rest of the scene. He sighs.*

**OSWIN:** Ah. Yes. The oath. I swore to guard this door until the Queen's light came home. The light is here. The oath still holds me. Oaths are rude like that.

**WICK:** I could fix that. I fix lamps. Mostly. How hard can a chain be?

*Wick raises the lantern toward the chain. The chain shines brighter. Behind him, the Sentry steps forward, one bony hand raised.*

**SENTRY:** Lamplighter. Wait. Before you do that, there is something I have not told you.

*Oswin lifts an eyebrow. Wick freezes with the lantern halfway up.*

**NARRATOR:** Wick could free the knight right now. Or he could hear what the Sentry had kept to himself for a hundred years.

## WHAT HAPPENS IN EPISODE 4

**If A wins (Free Oswin):**
Wick lifts the lantern and the chain cracks into drifting gold sparks. Oswin stands up, apologizes to his own knees, and becomes the first party member. The Sentry salutes and stays to guard the door. With Oswin free, the stair rebuilds itself and the party climbs out into Emberfall at dawn. Four lamps are still dark. Oswin is shocked at how small the village is now. The Gloomhound that did not run is waiting by the chapel, and Oswin has strong opinions about it. A peddler in the background (Quillon Fenn) quietly watches them go by. Ending choice: hide Oswin from Elder Bram, or walk straight up and show Bram.

**If B wins (Hear the Sentry first):**
The Sentry explains that the night the Sunwell fell, the Queen herself sealed this door and ordered the two of them to protect what is inside. If Oswin leaves his post, the door is unguarded, and anyone with the key can walk in. He also admits the key was stolen from the Keepers long ago, and that someone who hunts the light has been waiting for it to come back. He shows Wick a small chalk mark of a snuffed flame hidden at the base of the door (new plant). Oswin quietly adds one thing the Sentry forgot. Wick now knows the cost of freeing him. Ending choice: free Oswin anyway and take the risk, or leave Oswin on guard and carry the Sentry's warning back up to Emberfall.
